Services

Servicing

Your clock or watch has stopped working? Does it keep stopping or run too slow? Old oil, dust or even skin flakes are often the problem. With a complete cleaning and fresh oil, even a timepiece you thought was dead could run again.

We will be happy to advise you.

A service includes:

dismantling and cleaning the movement, burnishing the pivots if necessary, reassembling and oiling the movement, followed by a test run

Servicing of Clocks

This service includes completely dismantling the clock, cleaning all parts and, if necessary, burnishing the pivots. After servicing, the clock is tested for accuracy and regulated over 4–7 days.

Every clock is unique. We will be happy to advise you and can collect the clock from you on request.

The Clock Art team is always a good partner for repairing your small and large treasures.

repairs such as teeth on wheels and barrels, replacing pivots and bearings, repairs to lantern pinions, springs, etc.

(also for watches).

We find the problem with your timepiece and get it running again.
